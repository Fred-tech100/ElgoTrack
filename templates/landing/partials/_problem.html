The everyday reality

Running a business shouldn't mean chasing scattered records.

Most businesses already work hard. The friction comes from the tools — or the lack of them.

  • Stock that doesn't add up

    You're not always sure what the real balance is until the shelves are already half empty.

  • Missing items, unclear reasons

    Stock goes down without an explanation — and without a way to trace what happened.

  • Customer debts slipping

    Credit given today becomes hard to track next month, especially across staff.

  • Records in too many places

    Notebooks, receipts, spreadsheets — none of them talk to each other.

  • Purchases and expenses scattered

    Hard to see where the money went and what's coming in next.

  • Guessing what sells

    Without clean data, decisions come down to memory and gut feeling.

ElgoTrack was built to bring these records together — in one clear, accountable place.
